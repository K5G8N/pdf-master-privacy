PDF Master Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PDF Master handles files, information on your device, ads, and purchases.

App: PDF Master (Android, com.doctoroc.pdfmaster)
Google Play developer name: Doctor OC Apps
Effective date: November 4, 2026
Privacy contact: doctoroc.apps@outlook.com

Scope and accounts

The app does not create user accounts. The developer does not upload PDFs or OCR-recognized text to a developer-operated server and does not keep a PDF archive on its server. Processing by Google services, the advertising SDK, and Android backup is described below.

Files and information processed on your device

You can open PDFs through Android's file picker, share them from another app, or open them from a web address you enter in the app. PDF viewing and the app's editing, hand-drawn signing, search, merge, split, conversion, and other operations run on your device. A web-address request goes to that web server, which may process connection information such as your IP address and request time under its own practices. The request is not sent to a developer-operated server.

The app may store information needed for its features in its local database, preference files, and private storage, including recent file names and locations, page count, file size, last-opened time and last viewed page; folder names and favorites; viewing and theme preferences; trash records; saved hand-drawn signature data and signature images already stored in the app; onboarding and in-app review state; Premium and reward status; and notification schedules.

Access to external files depends on Android's permissions and the action you choose. Files in the app's private storage are marked for deletion from the in-app trash after 30 days and are deleted during a trash cleanup after that period; a file may remain if a technical deletion error occurs. When an externally stored file is put in the app's trash, the original file is not moved or deleted; only its in-app record is kept.

Document scanning and OCR

Document scanning uses the Google ML Kit document scanner provided by Google Play services; the scanner allows camera scanning and importing images from your gallery. Scanning and document processing take place on the device; output may be copied into the app's private storage. Google Play services may download scanner components and models and process technical information needed to operate its service.

OCR (recognizing text in an image) uses the Google ML Kit text-recognition library on the device. The image, PDF contents, and recognized text are not sent to the developer. Google states that ML Kit inputs and outputs are not sent to Google servers; the APIs may communicate with Google servers for bug fixes, model compatibility, and performance, usage, and diagnostic metrics. See ML Kit Terms and Privacy and ML Kit Android data disclosure.

Advertising, consent, and technical data

The free version may show banner, app-open, rewarded, and other ad formats through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google says the SDK may automatically collect and share IP addresses, advertising and other device/app identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic/performance data with Google or advertising partners for ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, and diagnostics. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app does not show ads while Premium ad-removal access is active.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to request and manage ad privacy choices where required. If UMP requires it, the app shows Settings > Files and privacy > Ad privacy options. Ad personalization depends on your location and choices. You can manage or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled through Google Play Billing. The app uses product IDs and purchase status to enable or restore Premium access. PDF Master does not receive your payment card or bank details; Google Play handles payment and transaction records.

Sharing, export, and notifications

You initiate file sharing, printing, exporting, or opening a file in another app. The file you select may be transferred to the destination app through Android's sharing or file-access mechanisms. The destination app's data practices are governed by its own policy.

Optional Premium promotion or reward reminder notifications may be scheduled locally. The schedule and related preferences are stored on the device; notification records are not sent to the developer. Notifications depend on Android permissions and settings.

Retention, deletion, backups, and security

Recent-file records and settings may stay on your device until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. The app allows Android backup. Depending on your Android version, backup settings, and selected provider, app-private files, its database, and preferences—including PDFs created in the app and saved signatures—may be included in a device backup. Android and your selected provider control backup retention, restore, and deletion; clearing app data does not necessarily delete existing backups. You can manage backup in device settings.

Technical data sent to Google advertising, scanning, ML Kit, and Google Play services is subject to those providers' retention, security, and deletion practices. Providers may process data in locations outside your country.

Privacy choices and requests

The developer does not keep user accounts or PDF/OCR content on a developer-operated server. Delete app data stored on your device through Android settings. Use the relevant Google account, advertising, and privacy controls for information and ad preferences processed by Google advertising or Play services. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, or to object to processing. Contact doctoroc.apps@outlook.com; requests for data held by third-party providers may need to be made to them.

Children and audience

PDF Master is a general-purpose PDF app and is not specifically designed to target children. Google Play audience and advertising settings should reflect the app's actual features and promotion. Additional Google Play rules and children's privacy laws may apply if children are included in the target audience.

Changes and contact

This policy may change if app features, SDKs, or applicable requirements change. The current version is published on this page and linked from the app settings.

Privacy questions and requests: doctoroc.apps@outlook.com.
