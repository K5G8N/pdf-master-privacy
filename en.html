PDF Master Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PDF Master handles information, what it stores on your device, and the role of third-party services.

Developer: Doctor OC Apps
Effective date: October 14, 2026
Privacy contact: doctoroc.apps@outlook.com

Scope

This policy applies to PDF Master for Android (package name: com.doctoroc.pdfmaster). The app does not create user accounts. The developer does not upload PDFs opened in the app or text recognized by OCR to a developer-operated server and does not maintain a PDF archive on its own server.

Files and information stored on your device

PDFs are opened through Android's file picker and supported operations are performed on your device. The app can download a file from a web address you enter; in that case, a request is sent to that address. Scanned output and copies created by the app are stored on your device. Access to external files depends on Android permissions and the action you choose.

The app may store information needed for its features on your device, including recent file names or locations, folders, favorites, viewing preferences, recycle-bin records, saved signature drawings, and Premium/reward status. Copies kept in the app's recycle bin are designed to be automatically deleted after 30 days. Clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data.

Scanning and OCR

Document scanning uses the scanning component provided by Google Play services; OCR (text recognition from images) uses the Google ML Kit text-recognition library. The app does not send scanned PDFs or recognized text to the developer to perform these features. Google services may process technical information to operate, secure, and diagnose their services, subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

Advertising and technical data

The free version may display ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK may automatically collect and share data such as IP address, advertising or device/app identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, and diagnostics. Processing is subject to Google's and relevant providers' policies. When the Premium ad-removal feature is active, the app is designed not to display ads.

Ad personalization and consent options may vary by country and by the choices you make. You can manage advertising preferences in Android settings. Details: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and Premium

In-app purchases and any subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. The app uses purchase status to unlock Premium features and restore access when appropriate. PDF Master does not collect payment card or bank details; payments are handled by Google Play. Purchase records are subject to Google's privacy policy.

Sharing and export

You initiate file sharing, exporting, printing, or opening a file in another app. The file you select may be transferred to the app you choose through Android's sharing or file-access mechanisms. The recipient app's data practices are governed by its own policy.

Retention and security

The developer does not operate a server that hosts user accounts or a PDF archive. The security of files on your device also depends on your device lock, operating-system permissions, backup settings, and the apps with which you share files. Technical information shared with Google advertising, scanning, OCR, and Google Play services is subject to those providers' retention and security practices.

Privacy choices and your rights

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app; this may not delete files saved elsewhere. Use the relevant Google account, advertising, and privacy controls for data and ad preferences processed by advertising SDKs or Google Play. Applicable laws may give you rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, or to object to processing. Requests to delete records held by third parties are subject to those providers' processes.

Children and audience

PDF Master is a general-purpose document productivity app and is not designed specifically to target children. Google Play audience and advertising settings should reflect the app's actual features and store listing. Additional rules and children's privacy laws may apply if children are included in the target audience.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if app features, software components, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact: doctoroc.apps@outlook.com.

This policy has been prepared with regard to the features of PDF Master and the SDKs it uses.
